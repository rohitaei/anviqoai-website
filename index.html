How Anvi works

Observe → understand → correlate → predict → assist.

Anvi is designed as a decision-support intelligence layer. It does not pretend to replace plant control systems or human authority.

ObserveAuthorized signals, alarms, states and records.
UnderstandAsset identity, operating context and relationships.
CorrelateEvents, history and evidence across equipment.
PredictEarly warning, risk and likely failure patterns.
AssistExplainable actions for people to verify and execute.

Supervisor

ANVI

“I found the significant parameter changes, new alarms, equipment state changes and verified maintenance actions. I have ranked them by operational importance.”
